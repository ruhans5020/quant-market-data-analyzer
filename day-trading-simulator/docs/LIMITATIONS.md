# Limitations

This is a paper-trading research project, not an exchange simulator.

- Historical data can be delayed, incomplete, adjusted, or unavailable.
- Bid/ask quotes are estimated rather than sourced from a live order book.
- Slippage and partial fills are simplified models.
- Queue position, hidden liquidity, routing, market impact, and exchange matching are not modeled.
- Simulated results are educational and are not evidence that a trading strategy will be profitable.
