# Mathematical Background

Simple return: r_t = P_t/P_(t-1) - 1

Annualized volatility: sigma = daily_sigma * sqrt(252)

Sharpe ratio: mean(excess return) / standard deviation(excess return) * sqrt(252)

Maximum drawdown measures the largest peak-to-trough decline in cumulative wealth.

Z-score: (x - rolling mean) / rolling standard deviation.

These statistics describe historical behavior; they do not predict future returns.
